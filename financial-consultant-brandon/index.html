A useful checklist before you meet

You don't need a complicated packet to begin. Spend a few minutes collecting the information and questions most relevant to the choice you're weighing:

  • Describe the investment decision or concern in a sentence.
  • Note which goals you want the decision to support.
  • Write down what level of volatility feels difficult to accept.
  • Gather any investment details you have that relate to the discussion.

Try not to make a change based only on a brief market reaction, and don't assume that a decision that suited you in the past still fits automatically. If something isn't clear, mark it as a question instead of trying to resolve it on your own. That can make the meeting more focused.
