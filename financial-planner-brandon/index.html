How to prepare for a planning conversation

You can make your first conversation easier to follow with a brief, practical checklist:

  • Write down the decision you are facing and why it is on your mind now.
  • Name the goal or priority you want the decision to support.
  • Note what kinds of market changes would make you uncomfortable.
  • Gather relevant investment information you already have, but do not delay a call because a document is missing.

During the discussion, listen for how your stated priorities and risk comfort are being used to evaluate investment choices. Ask what role volatility plays in the process. A financial consultant can help explain considerations, but you should not assume that any investment can eliminate market uncertainty or guarantee a particular outcome.
